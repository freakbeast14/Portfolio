MatSim is a finite‑element-analysis (FEA) simulation workspace that brings materials, geometries, and solver runs into one place so you can upload CAD/STL shapes, configure boundary conditions and material properties, run stress‑strain analyses, and then explore results with charts, mesh previews, and side‑by‑side comparisons across multiple tests.
